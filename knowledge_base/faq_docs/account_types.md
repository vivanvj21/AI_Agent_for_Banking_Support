# Account Types We Offer

- **Checking**: Everyday spending account, no minimum balance, linked debit card.
- **Savings**: Interest-bearing account, limited free withdrawals per month.
- **Credit**: Revolving credit line; balance shown represents amount currently owed, not
  available funds. Minimum payment is due monthly to avoid late fees.

You can hold multiple accounts of different types under one profile.
# Checking Your Balance and Statements

Your current balance reflects all posted transactions. Pending transactions (e.g. a purchase
that hasn't fully cleared) may not yet be reflected. Statements are generated monthly and
available for the past 24 months. For a real-time list of recent transactions, ask for your
"transaction history" rather than waiting for the monthly statement.
# Lost or Stolen Card

If your debit or credit card is lost or stolen, lock it immediately through the app or by
contacting support. Locking a card is instant and reversible — you can unlock it later if you
find the card. Reporting it as lost/stolen is a separate, permanent action that triggers a
replacement card to be issued within 5-7 business days.

You are not liable for fraudulent transactions made after you report the card lost or stolen,
provided you report within 3 days of noticing the unauthorized activity.
# Disputes and Refunds

If a charge looks correct but you're unhappy with a purchase (e.g. wrong item, service not
rendered), that's a merchant dispute, not fraud — the process differs. Merchant disputes take
up to 45 days to resolve and require the merchant to respond. Fraud reports (transactions you
never made) are typically faster, often resolved within 10 business days pending investigation.
