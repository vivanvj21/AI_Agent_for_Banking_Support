# Reporting a Fraudulent Transaction

To report a transaction as fraudulent, provide the transaction ID (found in your statement or
transaction history) and a brief description of why it looks suspicious. Once reported:

1. The transaction is flagged and a provisional credit may be issued within 10 business days.
2. An investigation is opened; you may be contacted for additional details.
3. If confirmed fraudulent, the credit becomes permanent and the case is closed.

If you notice multiple suspicious transactions, report each one individually so each can be
investigated on its own merits.
